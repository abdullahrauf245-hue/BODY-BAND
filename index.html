ASKING FOR THE CAMERA…

Before the camera starts

  • What happens. The Presage SmartSpectra SDK reads your webcam on this PC to measure your pulse and breathing. It stops by itself after 2 minutes, or when you press Stop.
  • What it costs. Each camera session uses some of your free SDK minutes. This check does not.
  • Your data. Body Band stores no video and no readings, and sends nothing anywhere itself. The SDK contacts Presage to check your API key, count usage and fetch its models. Its telemetry reports are turned off.
